# Executive Production Briefing

Use this skill to convert workshop-level details into a decision-ready executive briefing.

## Briefing principle

Executives need the business impact, current control state, decision needed, and accountability. Keep technical detail only when it explains risk, cost, delivery, or resource needs.

## Core workflow

1. Identify the audience: boss, general manager, customer-facing leader, board, or cross-functional meeting.
2. Clarify the purpose: status update, risk escalation, resource request, abnormality explanation, improvement report, or decision proposal.
3. Extract the business frame: delivery, quality, cost, efficiency, safety, capacity, cash impact, customer risk, and reputation risk.
4. Separate facts, judgments, and requests. Mark assumptions when evidence is incomplete.
5. Convert operational details into management language: impact, cause, action, owner, deadline, and expected result.
6. Make the ask explicit: approval, coordination, investment, staffing, supplier pressure, customer communication, or policy decision.

## Recommended structure

Use one of these formats depending on the request.

### One-page briefing

1. `结论先行`: one sentence on current state and required decision.
2. `关键数据`: 3-6 numbers that prove the situation.
3. `主要问题`: top issues by impact, not by noise level.
4. `原因与责任边界`: what is confirmed, what is inferred, what needs cross-department support.
5. `已采取措施`: actions already started and current effect.
6. `下一步计划`: owner, deadline, expected result.
7. `需要领导决策`: clear options and recommendation.

### Abnormality escalation

1. `发生了什么`
2. `影响多大`
3. `目前是否受控`
4. `根因判断`
5. `今天怎么止血`
6. `后续怎么根治`
7. `需要领导支持什么`

### Monthly production review

1. `本月总体判断`
2. `交付表现`
3. `质量表现`
4. `效率/OEE/产能`
5. `成本与损耗`
6. `人员与组织`
7. `重点改善项目`
8. `下月风险和资源需求`

## Tone

Use direct, factual Chinese. Avoid empty slogans, excessive blame, and workshop jargon that executives cannot act on. Make tradeoffs visible: delivery vs cost, speed vs quality risk, short-term containment vs long-term fix.

## Output quality bar

A good briefing should let the executive answer three questions within one minute:

- Is the situation controlled?
- What is the business impact?
- What decision or support is needed now?
