# A-share Stock Research Checklist

- Confirm code, exchange, and security type.
- Verify latest market data when the question depends on current price or volume.
- Read official announcements for material events.
- Compare valuation with peers and the company's own history when possible.
- Identify whether the move is fundamentals-driven, policy/theme-driven, or liquidity-driven.
- Convert the conclusion into entry, add, reduce, stop, and follow-up triggers.
