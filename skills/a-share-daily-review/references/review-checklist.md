# A-share Daily Review Checklist

- Confirm date, session state, and whether data is live, delayed, or user-provided.
- Check major indices and total market turnover first.
- Check market breadth before making an index-level conclusion.
- Identify the leading theme by both strength and follow-through, not by news heat alone.
- Note failed themes and high-crowding areas.
- Convert analysis into triggers: confirm, invalidate, watch, avoid.
- Keep position suggestions conditional and risk-aware.
